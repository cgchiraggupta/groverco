PRIVACY / PLAIN-LANGUAGE NOTICE

Your information.
Handled with care.

This notice explains what happens when you use the Grover & Co. website, including forms for Grover Strategy House and Works by Nish.

What you may share

Depending on the form, this can include your name, phone number, email address, business or creator name, profile links, industry, location, audience, the source or channel you selected, collaboration preferences, budget or rates, and the information you add to a service or campaign enquiry. Please do not send passwords, identity documents, bank details or other sensitive information through these forms.

Why we use it

We use your information to respond to enquiries, consider creator applications, review business collaboration requests, make opt-in match suggestions, provide the free guide you selected, and manage follow-up. The team may record the source of an enquiry, contact attempts, follow-up status and internal notes. Submitting a form does not guarantee a service, campaign or collaboration.

Matching is optional

Creator and business forms include a separate optional choice to share relevant profile or brief details with a suitable potential collaborator. We only prepare an introduction when both sides have opted in and an administrator has reviewed the match. Contact details are included only in that reviewed introduction.

Where information goes

Form submissions are stored in a private Supabase database for the Grover & Co. administrator to review. The website is hosted by Vercel. The site also forwards form details through FormSubmit to worksbynish@gmail.com, with a copy to chiraggupta0999@gmail.com, so enquiries can be seen by the team. These providers process information to deliver the website, database or email service.

Storage and deletion

Dashboard records are kept until they are no longer needed or a deletion request is handled. The administrator can remove records from the dashboard. FormSubmit may retain submissions in its archive for up to 30 days. Removing a dashboard record does not remove copies already delivered to an email inbox.

Your choices

You can leave the optional matching choice unchecked. You can ask to access, correct or delete a submission by emailing worksbynish@gmail.com or chiraggupta0999@gmail.com. We may need enough information to identify the submission before making a change.

Questions

For questions about this notice or a form submission, contact either email above. This page describes the current website setup and is not legal advice.
