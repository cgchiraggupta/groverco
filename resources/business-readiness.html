FREE GUIDE / BUSINESS

Business readiness
checklist.

Use this checklist to organise your goals, information and next steps before speaking with a consultant, finance partner or marketing team. You do not need to have every answer today.

Print this page or choose “Save as PDF” in your browser’s print dialog.

1. Be clear about the business

  • Write down what you sell and who it is for.
  • List your main products or services and how customers buy them.
  • Note your business structure and who makes key decisions.
  • Gather your current business and contact details in one place.

2. Define the next goal

  • Choose one goal for the next 90 days.
  • Describe what success would look like and how you will recognise it.
  • List the main challenge slowing progress today.
  • Separate urgent needs from improvements that can wait.

3. Organise the numbers

  • Collect recent sales, expense and cash-flow information you already maintain.
  • List major upcoming payments and expected receipts.
  • Note questions about working capital, reporting or financial planning.
  • Share only what is necessary with an appropriate professional; do not send passwords or identity documents through a website enquiry form.

4. Review the customer journey

  • Write down how a new customer discovers you, enquires, buys and receives support.
  • Identify where customers commonly pause or need more information.
  • Collect a few examples of questions customers ask.
  • Check that your website, business page and contact details are current.

5. Choose a manageable next step

  • Pick one action that can be completed in the next two weeks.
  • Assign an owner and a date.
  • Decide what information you need before taking that action.
  • Review progress at the end of the period and adjust the next step.

Bring to your first conversation

Your goal, a short description of the business, the challenge you want to solve, the information you already have, your preferred timeline and the questions you want answered.
