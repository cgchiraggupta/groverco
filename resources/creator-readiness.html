Creator collaboration
readiness checklist.

Use this guide to make your creator profile easier to understand and prepare for a clear, professional brand conversation.

Print this page or choose “Save as PDF” in your browser’s print dialog.

1. Make your profile easy to review

  • List your creator name and primary social profile links.
  • Choose the content categories and topics you can represent credibly.
  • Describe the audience you reach using information available in your platform insights.
  • Keep a few examples of recent work or a portfolio link ready.

2. Know your formats

  • List the formats you can deliver, such as short video, stories, photos or long-form video.
  • Note any production strengths, languages or locations relevant to your work.
  • Be specific about what is included in each format.
  • Keep your profile information current when your content focus changes.

3. Prepare for a clear collaboration

  • Think about the types of brands and campaigns that fit your audience.
  • Set out your availability and the locations you can cover.
  • Prepare your rate card or expected range if you use one.
  • Ask about deliverables, timelines, usage, revisions and payment before agreeing to work.

4. Keep your process organised

  • Use a dedicated business contact method and check it regularly.
  • Confirm campaign details and deadlines in writing.
  • Keep copies of approved briefs and final deliverables.
  • Follow applicable platform rules and disclosure requirements for sponsored content.

Before you opt into a match

Review the details you are comfortable sharing with a potential business. Grover & Co. uses an optional match-sharing choice, reviews suggestions manually and prepares any introduction for review before it is sent.
